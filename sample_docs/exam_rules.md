# Examination Rules and Code of Conduct

**Maple Ridge Academy** (a fictional school created for this demo project)

## 1. Examination Schedule
The school holds two terminal examinations each year: the Mid-Term Examination in October
and the Final Examination in March. Monthly class tests are held in the last week of each month.

## 2. Attendance Requirement
A student must have at least 75% attendance in the term to be allowed to sit the terminal
examination. The Principal may relax this rule for documented medical reasons.

## 3. Reporting Time
Students must be seated in the examination hall 15 minutes before the paper starts.
A student who arrives more than 30 minutes late will not be allowed to enter the hall.

## 4. Permitted Items
Students may bring blue or black pens, a pencil, an eraser, a ruler and a transparent water
bottle. Simple calculators are allowed only in Grade 9 and above for Mathematics and Physics.
Mobile phones and smart watches are strictly not allowed in the examination hall.

## 5. Unfair Means
A student caught cheating or using unfair means will receive zero marks in that paper.
The parents will be called to the school, and a repeated offence may lead to suspension.

## 6. Missed Examinations
A student who misses an examination because of illness must submit a medical certificate
within 3 days. A retest may be arranged at the discretion of the Examination Coordinator.

## 7. Results
Results are shared on the parent portal within 14 days of the last paper. Requests for
rechecking must be made in writing within 5 days of the result and cost 500 PKR per subject.
