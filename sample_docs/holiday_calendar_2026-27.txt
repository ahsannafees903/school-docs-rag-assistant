MAPLE RIDGE ACADEMY - HOLIDAY CALENDAR 2026-27
(Maple Ridge Academy is a fictional school created for this demo project.)

The academic year begins on Monday, 3 August 2026 and ends on Friday, 28 May 2027.

Summer break: 1 June 2026 to 2 August 2026. School reopens on 3 August 2026.

Independence Day: 14 August 2026 (school closed).

Mid-term break: 26 October 2026 to 28 October 2026, after the Mid-Term Examination.

Winter break: 21 December 2026 to 3 January 2027. School reopens on Monday, 4 January 2027.

Kashmir Day: 5 February 2027 (school closed).

Pakistan Day: 23 March 2027 (school closed).

Spring break: 29 March 2027 to 2 April 2027, after the Final Examination.

Eid holidays are announced separately based on the official moon-sighting decision.

Parent-Teacher Meetings are held on the first Saturday after each terminal examination result.
