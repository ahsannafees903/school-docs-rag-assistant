# Admission Policy 2026-27

**Maple Ridge Academy** (a fictional school created for this demo project)

## 1. Admission Windows
Admissions for the 2026-27 academic year open on 1 February 2026 and close on 31 March 2026.
Late applications are accepted only if seats remain, and are reviewed after 15 April 2026.

## 2. Age Requirements
A child applying for Kindergarten must be at least 4 years old on 1 August 2026.
A child applying for Grade 1 must be at least 6 years old on 1 August 2026.

## 3. Required Documents
Parents must submit the following documents with the admission form:
- A copy of the child's birth certificate.
- Two recent passport-size photographs of the child.
- The previous school's leaving certificate and last report card (Grade 2 and above).
- Proof of residence, such as a utility bill.
- A copy of the parent or guardian's identity card.

## 4. Entrance Assessment
Applicants for Grade 2 to Grade 8 take a short entrance assessment in English, Mathematics
and Science. The assessment takes 60 minutes and is held on the second Saturday of April.
Kindergarten and Grade 1 applicants attend a 15-minute informal interaction instead of a test.

## 5. Sibling Priority
Siblings of currently enrolled students receive priority, provided the application is
submitted before the closing date of 31 March 2026.

## 6. Confirmation of Admission
Admission is confirmed only after the admission fee is paid within 7 days of the offer
letter. Offers not confirmed within 7 days may be given to the next applicant on the waiting list.
